# #608: Standardize the usage of "serialize" over "serialise"

- **State:** open
- **Author:** picu63
- **Created:** 2025-04-03
- **Original URL:** https://github.com/aloneguid/parquet-dotnet/issues/608
- **Recovery status:** Recovered

## Description

### Issue description

I've noticed that within the Parquet .NET library, the terms "serialize" and "serialise" are used interchangeably in some places. This inconsistency can lead to confusion and reduce the overall readability of the code.
I would like to propose a discussion to standardize all instances of the term to "serialize" (with a 'z') for the sake of consistency and to follow the convention more commonly used in technical documentation and programming.
Looking forward to your thoughts and contributions on this matter!
Thank you.
