### dmunch on 2025-02-01

I've had a brief look into this, basically, currently the binary distributed is not completely self-contained, but also includes some Avalon-related `.dylib` files, which each one has to be added one after another to the Gatekeeper exceptions.

<img width="574" alt="Image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1e31e344-bbf8-4703-96fd-727e12db59ce" />

Adding an additional `<IncludeAllContentForSelfExtract>true</IncludeAllContentForSelfExtract>` to the `.csproj` files fixes it, and adds these files as well, making the Gatekeeper exceptions less of a hassle. (Mentioned in https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia/issues/11710).

There's also nice docs about publishing an Avalonia app as an actual `.app` bundle with code-signing, but that's probably over the top and also requires a paid  Apple developer subscription. (The signing; the .app bundle might be a nice intermediary step to get a nice logo on the app and avoid the terminal window which automatically pops up when launching floor): https://docs.avaloniaui.net/docs/deployment/macOS
